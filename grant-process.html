District 3790 · Grants Subcommittees

Two grant tracks, two different paths through the District.

District Grants and Global Grants are administered differently under the District 3790 Stewardship Plan. This page walks through each process end to end, and how to tell which one your project belongs on.

Start here

Which track fits your project?

The choice isn't made by the District — TRF's grant structure decides it

Scale & funding: District Grants draw only on the District's annual DDF allocation and bundle into one consolidated submission. Global Grants require a minimum total budget (historically $30,000 including the World Fund match) and are individually submitted to TRF with their own grant number.

Scope & partners: District Grants are short-cycle, single-Rotary-year activities. Global Grants are larger, sustainability-driven, and generally need a host sponsor plus an international partner club or district.

If a club is unsure which fits, that's a triage conversation with the Grants Chair or Global Grant Chair before either process below begins.

Step by step

The process, once you know your track

Both tracks run through DGRAC — the District Grant Review and Approval Committee — but the approval gate differs sharply after that.

01

Club submits application

To the Grants Chair — not to DGRAC directly. Package includes project description, proposed budget, signed Club MOU, and proof of GMS attendance.

Sec. 6.2
02

Grants Chair review

Checks completeness, eligibility, purpose, budget, and District/TRF compliance. Incomplete applications are returned to the club for correction, not held.

Sec. 6.3
03

DGRAC review

Weighs eligibility, community need, Areas-of-Focus alignment, budget reasonableness, available funds, equitable access, and the club's prior compliance. Recommendation is documented.

Sec. 6.4
04

District approval

The appropriate District Foundation decision-making body signs off before the application moves to consolidation.

Sec. VI
05

Consolidate and submit to TRF

Applications must exist before the spending plan — never the reverse. The District compiles every approved club application into one consolidated submission to RI/TRF.

Sec. 6.6–6.7
06

TRF approval and release

Funds are distributed matching each club's approved allocation. The Treasurer releases only after approvals and documentation are confirmed — minimum two signatories, District Governor excluded.

Sec. 6.8, 10.3
07

Implementation and monitoring

Grants Chair tracks progress. Any change in scope, budget, or beneficiaries needs a written request and DGRAC referral before it's implemented — never verbal-only.

Sec. 6.9–6.10
08

Reporting and closure

Required reports are filed. Unused funds of $1,000 or less can be repurposed without preapproval; above that, TRF approval is needed before use or the funds are returned.

Sec. XIX–XX
01

Club submits concept

To the Global Grant Chair — project description, international partner, budget, and the DDF amount being requested, if any.

Sec. VII
02

Qualification and priority screen

Confirms TRF qualification (MOU, GMS) and applies priority rules: clubs with an existing DDF-supported Global Grant can't submit a second one; prior-year DDF recipients rank lower.

Sec. 5.1, 7.2–7.3
03

DGRAC review

Same committee as District Grants, reviewing the same criteria — recommends the DDF request forward.

Sec. 6.4
04

DDF concurrence

Written, unanimous sign-off from the District Governor, Governor-elect, and Rotary Foundation Chair. No 2-of-3 substitute, no verbal approval. This is the one gate with no workaround.

Sec. 7.1
05

DDF recorded

Finance Chair or DRFC logs the commitment in the DDF Monitoring Register before it's treated as allocated.

Appendix D
06

Submit to TRF

The club finalizes and submits the Global Grant application directly in Grant Center on My Rotary, with District endorsement attached.

Sec. VII
07

Monitoring

District tracks status — pending, approved, active, closed — through to TRF activation and beyond.

Sec. 3.6
08

Closure and report

Final report filed. Unused funds of $1,000 or less can be repurposed without preapproval; above that, TRF approval is needed before use or the funds are returned.

Sec. XX
Before you route a request

Rules that block or reorder a request

Apply these checks in either track before anything advances past DGRAC.

Governor's own club
Categorically ineligible for any District Grant during that Governor's term — no recusal cure. Sec. 6.5-A
Other DGRAC member's club
Eligible, but last priority after other clubs are considered; the member recuses from the decision. Sec. 6.5-B/C
Sequencing
The spending plan is built from actual applications — never drafted first and backfilled with applications. Sec. 6.6
No unapproved reallocation
Funds approved for one project can't shift to another without formal approval. Sec. 6.9
Unanimous concurrence
Any DDF request needs written sign-off from DG, DGE, and DRFC together — not a majority. Sec. 7.1
One Global Grant at a time
A club with an existing DDF-supported Global Grant can't be considered for a second one in the same period, and can't reserve DDF for a future grant. Sec. 7.3
Compliance gate
Overdue reports or unresolved misuse findings block new DDF allocation until resolved. Sec. 7.4
Cross-district requests
District 3790 will not fund another district's club's Global Grant unless that district already committed its own DDF, and never for anything returned to D3790 or its officers. Sec. 8.10
